# Community leadership and ambassador program

## Purpose

The leadership network exists to educate members, run safe local activities, improve
support, and carry community feedback into WRS. It is not a sales downline and does not
grant a claim on member purchases or earnings.

## Progression model

| Role | Purpose | Typical evidence | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Member | Own and train a robot profile; learn and participate | verified account and community-rule acceptance | self |
| Contributor | Create useful AI training data | accepted work and contributor standing | assigned tasks |
| Ambassador | Educate, welcome, and organize | training, conduct record, supported events/members | assigned program/community |
| City Leader | Coordinate active city operations | sustained ambassador performance and local need | one city |
| State Leader | Support multiple active cities | operating need and successful city leadership | one state/province |
| Country Director | Coordinate national programs | legal/operational approval and measurable demand | one country |
| Regional Director | Align several countries | executive appointment and active regional operation | named region |
| Global Ambassador | Represent WRS in a defined international mandate | exceptional record and executive approval | time-bound global mandate |

Progression is not automatic. Higher roles are created only when the lower-level
network and operating workload justify them. Package tier, referrals alone, or payment
cannot qualify a member for office.

## Role lifecycle

`Eligible → Applied/Nominated → Screened → Trained → Appointed → Active → Reviewed → Renewed/Ended`

Every appointment has:

- a named approver and geographic/program scope;
- required safeguarding, privacy, brand, event, and anti-fraud training;
- start/end dates and periodic review;
- a code of conduct and conflict-of-interest declaration;
- suspension and appeal procedures;
- an auditable permission grant and revocation.

## Responsibilities and boundaries

### Ambassador

- welcome and educate members;
- host approved events and record attendance;
- share official material and route support issues;
- collect structured feedback;
- never promise earnings, sell unofficial packages, collect passwords/OTP, or approve
  their own rewards.

### City Leader

- coordinate local meeting schedule, ambassador coverage, and onboarding quality;
- manage an event calendar and escalate safety/support incidents;
- monitor aggregate health metrics;
- never access member wallet details, identity documents, private contributions, or
  unrestricted personal data.

Higher levels coordinate leaders beneath their scope, manage operating quality, and
represent the community. They do not inherit unrestricted access to individual member
records.

## Dashboard

The dashboard uses minimum necessary, privacy-safe data:

- active members and new-member onboarding completion;
- training completion and aggregate contribution quality;
- events, attendance, feedback, and support resolution;
- referral qualification counts, never hidden compensation chains;
- aggregate robot activation/deployment status where appropriate;
- leader goals, credential expiry, conduct cases, and announcements;
- event code creation only for explicitly authorized roles.

Small cohorts must be suppressed or coarsened to avoid identifying individuals. Export
is off by default and requires a documented operational purpose.

## Event code controls

- codes are generated server-side for an approved event, organizer, time window, and
  geographic/device risk policy;
- five-to-ten-minute claim windows may be used, but the configured window is stored;
- one claim per eligible verified account; organizer self-approval is prohibited;
- rate limits, duplicate-device/account detection, and anomalous-location checks apply;
- reversal is possible and audited; a code proves platform attendance only, not legal
  identity or physical presence unless the event process explicitly supports that.

## Recognition and compensation

Recognition may include badges, certificates, learning access, event support, and
reimbursement. Any monetary compensation must be for a documented service or campaign,
have published eligibility and caps, and be recorded separately from referral rewards.
No role receives automatic percentage income from members beneath it.

## Success measures

- onboarding completion and member support resolution;
- repeat attendance and training completion;
- member satisfaction and leader response time;
- conduct, safety, fraud, and misleading-claim incident rates;
- retention adjusted for verified product activity, not recruitment volume.

## Phase plan

- **MVP:** community rules, announcements, events, staff-generated codes, basic
  moderation, and ambassador interest form.
- **Phase 2:** Ambassador and City Leader appointments, dashboards, scoped permissions,
  training, reviews, and safety cases.
- **Phase 3:** add higher geographic levels only as real operations require them;
  introduce formal Community Leadership Council charter.
