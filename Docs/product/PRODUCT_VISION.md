# WRS product vision

## Positioning

World Robotic System is a unified, mobile-first robotics and AI participation platform.
Members create a personalized digital robot profile, learn practical skills, train AI
with intentionally contributed data, participate in verified community work, and
become eligible for real opportunities as the network grows.

**Brand promise:** Own a Robot. Own the Future.

**Product promise:** Own a Robot. Train It. Deploy It. Build the Future.

The word **own** initially means control of a persistent digital robot profile and its
earned capabilities inside WRS. It must not imply title to a physical robot, an
investment security, or guaranteed access to commercial work.

## Core experience

**Own → Train → Contribute → Deploy → Monitor → Earn → Upgrade**

Every part of the product supports this one loop:

1. **Own:** create an account, select platform access, and activate a robot profile.
2. **Train:** personalize voice, languages, knowledge, movement, and workflows with
   explicit consent.
3. **Contribute:** complete structured AI data tasks and build a contributor record.
4. **Deploy:** request available digital or physical opportunities for which the robot
   and owner are eligible.
5. **Monitor:** see activity, quality, performance, consent, and financial status.
6. **Earn:** receive XP, credits, or confirmed monetary value only from clearly defined
   and verified programs.
7. **Upgrade:** unlock capabilities through learning, reputation, earned upgrades, or
   a clearly priced access tier.

## Primary users and jobs

| User                | Primary job                                                     |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Member/owner        | Understand what the robot can do and the best next action       |
| Contributor         | Find suitable tasks and build a trusted contribution record     |
| Validator           | Review work consistently and improve dataset quality            |
| Ambassador/leader   | Educate and support a healthy local community                   |
| Enterprise customer | License or commission traceable, consented, quality-scored data |
| Administrator       | Operate the platform safely with auditable decisions            |

The initial member is likely to use a mid-range Android phone, on an intermittent
connection, for short daily sessions. No robotics or AI expertise is assumed.

## Product principles

1. **One connected journey.** Robot, training, data, deployment, wallet, and community
   are capabilities of one product, not separate businesses.
2. **Verified activity creates value.** Passive ownership or package purchase does not
   create earnings.
3. **Quality outranks volume and package tier.** Contribution quality is the primary
   input to data opportunity and distribution decisions.
4. **Authority cannot be purchased.** Leadership and validation permissions require
   conduct, performance, training, and review.
5. **Consent is specific and reversible.** Sensitive data is never licensed because a
   user accepted a general terms checkbox.
6. **Status is explicit.** Estimated, pending, confirmed, promotional, and non-cash
   balances must never look interchangeable.
7. **The MVP earns trust before scale.** Claims and features appear only when the
   supporting operations, controls, and real demand exist.

## What WRS is not

- A passive-income, fixed-return, investment, or universal-income product.
- A promise that buying a package produces work, revenue, or a physical robot.
- A multi-level compensation system. Community titles do not create downline income.
- A marketplace for data collected without purpose-specific consent and provenance.
- A system where package price determines human credibility or approval authority.

## Success measures

The north-star behavior is a member returning to complete a meaningful next action and
understanding how it changed their robot or professional profile.

Guardrail metrics include:

- consent withdrawal and deletion completion time;
- accepted contribution rate and validator agreement rate;
- appeal overturn rate and quality incidents per released dataset;
- percentage of rewards tied to verified activity;
- leader/member support resolution and community safety incidents;
- withdrawal, payment, and dataset licensing reconciliation breaks;
- clear comprehension of package, reward, and earnings labels in user research.
