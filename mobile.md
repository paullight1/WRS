35. Developer Design Principle

The platform should never make users feel that they are navigating separate products.

Robot ownership, AI training, data creation, deployment, rewards, wallet activity, and community participation should all feel like parts of one connected journey.

The central product experience is:

Own → Train → Contribute → Deploy → Monitor → Earn → Upgrade

---

36. Important Product and Compliance Notes

The app should clearly separate:

- Package purchase
- Platform access
- Robot ownership representation
- Points and XP
- Estimated earnings
- Confirmed earnings
- Referral bonuses
- Data contribution rewards
- Real robot deployment revenue

Users must be shown clear risk disclosures and consent controls.

Any earnings should be tied to verified activity, approved data, real service revenue, or clearly defined promotional rewards.

Packages must not be presented as guaranteeing profits, fixed returns, or universal income.

Biometric, facial, voice, movement, and language data require explicit user consent, secure storage, deletion controls, and clear licensing terms.

World Robotic System should be presented as a unified platform where users can:

- Own a personalized digital robot profile
- Select a membership package

- Earn platform rewards from verified participation
