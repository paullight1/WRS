WRS AI Training & Data Economy

Every Robot Learns From Its Owner

World Robotic System is more than a robot ownership platform.

It is also a global AI training ecosystem where every member can help build the next generation of intelligent robots.

Each robot begins with a general AI model.

Its owner can then personalize it through continuous training and data contributions.

This creates a robot that better understands its owner's language, preferences, culture, and workflows.

---

Dynamic Event Codes

During official events, webinars, training sessions, or community activities, WRS generates a unique event code.

Example:

WRS-458721

The code remains active for approximately 5–10 minutes.

Users enter the code in the Rewards section of the app to claim participation points.

This encourages live participation while preventing abuse.

Developer Notes

The rewards system should encourage meaningful participation rather than passive holding. Platform points, XP, and upgrades should be tied to learning, engagement, and verified contributions. Any financial rewards shown in the application should always be based on transparent, real commercial activity performed through the platform and should never be presented as fixed or guaranteed investment returns.
